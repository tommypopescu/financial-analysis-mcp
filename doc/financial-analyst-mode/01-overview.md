# Financial Analyst Mode - Overview

## Introduction

The Financial Analyst Mode is a specialized Bob mode designed for comprehensive stock market analysis and investment decision-making. It combines technical analysis, risk assessment, and scenario planning to provide clear, actionable investment recommendations.

## Purpose

This mode is specifically designed for **medium-term investors** (3-12 months investment horizon) who seek:
- **Safe, diversified investments** with calculated risk
- **Clear, unequivocal verdicts** (BUY/SELL/HOLD)
- **Comprehensive technical analysis** using multiple indicators
- **Risk management strategies** with stop-loss and profit targets
- **Scenario-based planning** for different market conditions

## Key Features

### 1. Comprehensive Technical Analysis
- **7 Technical Indicators**: EMA, RSI, MACD, ADX, Stochastic, Bollinger Bands, MFI
- **Multi-timeframe Analysis**: Short, medium, and long-term perspectives
- **Trend Identification**: Golden Cross, Death Cross, trend strength
- **Momentum Assessment**: Overbought/oversold conditions

### 2. 5-Phase Analysis Workflow
1. **Data Collection**: Gather historical data and current prices
2. **Technical Analysis**: Calculate and interpret all indicators
3. **Scenario Development**: Bullish, bearish, and neutral scenarios
4. **Risk Assessment**: Stop-loss levels, position sizing, risk-reward ratios
5. **Verdict Formulation**: Clear BUY/SELL/HOLD recommendation

### 3. Investment Recommendations
- **Unequivocal Verdicts**: No ambiguous "maybe" recommendations
- **Entry Strategies**: Ideal entry points and timing
- **Exit Strategies**: Stop-loss levels and profit targets
- **Risk Management**: Position sizing and portfolio allocation
- **Timeframe Guidance**: Expected holding period

### 4. Scenario Planning
- **Bullish Scenario**: Best-case outcomes and probabilities
- **Bearish Scenario**: Worst-case risks and mitigation
- **Neutral Scenario**: Sideways movement strategies
- **Probability Assessment**: Likelihood of each scenario

## Target Audience

### Ideal For:

## Technical Indicators Explained

### 1. EMA (Exponential Moving Average)
- **EMA50**: 50-period moving average (medium-term trend)
- **EMA200**: 200-period moving average (long-term trend)
- **Golden Cross**: EMA50 crosses above EMA200 (bullish)
- **Death Cross**: EMA50 crosses below EMA200 (bearish)

### 2. RSI (Relative Strength Index)
- **Range**: 0-100
- **Overbought**: >70 (potential reversal down)
- **Oversold**: <30 (potential reversal up)
- **Neutral**: 30-70 (healthy momentum)

### 3. MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence)
- **MACD Line**: 12-period EMA - 26-period EMA
- **Signal Line**: 9-period EMA of MACD
- **Histogram**: MACD - Signal
- **Bullish**: MACD above signal line
- **Bearish**: MACD below signal line

### 4. ADX (Average Directional Index)
- **Range**: 0-100
- **Weak Trend**: <20
- **Moderate Trend**: 20-40
- **Strong Trend**: >40
- **Direction**: +DI vs -DI

### 5. Stochastic Oscillator
- **%K**: Fast line (current momentum)
- **%D**: Slow line (smoothed %K)
- **Overbought**: >80
- **Oversold**: <20

### 6. Bollinger Bands
- **Upper Band**: Middle + 2 standard deviations
- **Middle Band**: 20-period SMA
- **Lower Band**: Middle - 2 standard deviations
- **Squeeze**: Low volatility (potential breakout)
- **Expansion**: High volatility (trending)

### 7. MFI (Money Flow Index)
- **Range**: 0-100 (volume-weighted RSI)
- **Overbought**: >80
- **Oversold**: <20
- **Divergence**: Price vs MFI disagreement

## Analysis Methodology

### Signal Confirmation
Recommendations require **multiple indicator alignment**:
- **Strong BUY**: 5+ bullish indicators
- **BUY**: 3-4 bullish indicators
- **HOLD**: Mixed signals or neutral
- **SELL**: 3-4 bearish indicators
- **Strong SELL**: 5+ bearish indicators

### Risk-Reward Assessment
Every recommendation includes:
- **Entry Point**: Ideal price to enter position
- **Stop-Loss**: Maximum acceptable loss level
- **Target 1**: First profit-taking level
- **Target 2**: Extended profit target
- **Risk-Reward Ratio**: Minimum 2:1 required

### Scenario Probabilities
Each analysis includes three scenarios:
- **Bullish**: Probability + expected gains
- **Neutral**: Probability + sideways range
- **Bearish**: Probability + potential losses

## Limitations and Disclaimers

### What This Mode Does NOT Do
- **Fundamental analysis** (earnings, P/E ratios, etc.)
- **News analysis** (company announcements, market events)
- **Macroeconomic analysis** (interest rates, GDP, etc.)
- **Sector rotation** strategies
- **Options trading** strategies
- **Cryptocurrency** analysis

### Important Disclaimers
1. **Not Financial Advice**: This is educational analysis, not professional financial advice
2. **Past Performance**: Historical data doesn't guarantee future results
3. **Market Risk**: All investments carry risk of loss
4. **Personal Responsibility**: Users must make their own investment decisions
5. **Diversification**: Never invest more than you can afford to lose

### Known Limitations
- **Technical-only focus**: Ignores fundamentals
- **Medium-term bias**: Not optimized for day trading or long-term
- **Market conditions**: Works best in trending markets
- **Data dependency**: Requires sufficient historical data
- **No real-time data**: 15-20 minute delay from Yahoo Finance

## Getting Started

### Quick Start
1. Switch to Financial Analyst mode: `/mode financial-analyst`
2. Analyze a ticker: `analizeaza AAPL` or `analyze TLV.RO`
3. Review the comprehensive report
4. Make informed investment decision

### Best Practices
- **Start with watchlist**: Use `list tickers` to see available tickers
- **Understand indicators**: Read indicator explanations in report
- **Consider scenarios**: Don't just focus on the verdict
- **Respect stop-loss**: Always set and honor stop-loss levels
- **Diversify**: Don't put all capital in one position
